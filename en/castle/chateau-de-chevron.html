Château de Chevron

Castle in Mercury in the Savoie département of France · Mercury, France

Photo: Franck Zoccolo, CC BY-SA 3.0

About

The Château de Chevron is a redesigned and remodelled castle in the commune of Mercury in the Savoie département of France. It was owned by the powerful Quilliard family.

History

Pope Nicholas II was born in Chevron between 990 and 995. For two years during the Second World War, the Quilliard family, owners of the château, hid a Jewish family of ten there. All survived. In 1996, the owners of the castle were given the title Righteous among the Nations by Yad Vashem and the state of Israel in recognition of their courage.
