Château de la Celle

French castle · La Celle-Saint-Cloud, France

Photo: Wikimedia Commons, Public domain

About

The Château de la Celle, also named Château de La Celle Saint-Cloud or the Petit Château, is a historical building in the suburban commune of La Celle-Saint-Cloud, in the French department of Yvelines, west of Paris, 6 kilometres (3.7 mi) north of Versailles. It is owned by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, which uses it as a diplomatic venue.
