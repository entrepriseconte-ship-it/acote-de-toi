Château de Nobles

Castle in France · La Chapelle-sous-Brancion, France

Photo: GFreihalter, CC BY-SA 4.0

About

The Château de Nobles is a castle in the French commune of La Chapelle-sous-Brancion, in the department of Saône-et-Loire. The castle is privately owned and is not open to the public. It has been listed since 1946 as a monument historique by the French Ministry of Culture.

History

1370 : Antoine de Nanton, who belonged to a Mâcon family, was the first lord known in the area. End of 16th century : Philiberte de Feurs, widow of François de Nanton, the last of his line, took the manor on remarriage to Jean de La Baume whose family held the property until the end of the Ancien Régime. Revolution : the castle was looted and partially destroyed. 1856 : during restoration work, what was left of the fortifications was demolished.
