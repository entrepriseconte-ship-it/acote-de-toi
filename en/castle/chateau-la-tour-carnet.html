Château La Tour Carnet

Bordeaux wine estate · Saint-Laurent-Médoc, France

Photo: Geographer, CC BY-SA 4.0

About

Château La Tour Carnet is a Bordeaux wine estate in the appellation Haut-Médoc. The wine produced here was classified as one of ten Quatrièmes Crus in the historic Bordeaux Wine Official Classification of 1855, at the time known by the name of Carnet.

History

The estate has origins in the early Middle Ages, though the details are largely unknown, but takes its name from Jean Caranet or Carnet, an heir of Jean de Foix who is believed responsible for having built the château tower. The estate has been owned by Bernard Magrez since 1999.
