Château du Sart

Castle · Villeneuve-d'Ascq, France

Photo: Jiel Beaumadier (http://jiel.b.free.fr), CC BY-SA 3.0

About

The Château du Sart is a château in the Sart-Babylone quarter of Villeneuve d'Ascq, the Nord department of France.

History

The château was built in 1740. It was rarely occupied by its owners, the Seigneurs of Fourmestraux. The park was transformed into an 18-hole golf course, and nowadays, the château has become the course club house.
