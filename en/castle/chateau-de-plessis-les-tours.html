Château de Plessis-lez-Tours

Castle · La Riche, France

Photo: Yannick Bonnet (yannick.bonnet@laposte.net), CC BY-SA 2.5

About

The royal Château de Plessis-lèz-Tours is the remains of a late Gothic château located in the town of La Riche in the Indre-et-Loire department, in the Loire Valley of France. Around three quarters of the former royal residence were pulled down during the French Revolution in 1796.
