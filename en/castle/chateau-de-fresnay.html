Château de Fresnay

Castle in Pays de la Loire, France · Le Bourgneuf-la-Forêt, France

Photo: Pymouss, CC BY-SA 3.0

About

The Château de Fresnay or Frênay is a French castle, 1.2 km to the north of Le Bourgneuf-la-Forêt in Mayenne. It is mentioned by Hubert Jaillot, on the Carte de Cassini, and by Pierre-François Davelu.
