About

The Château de Verteuil, also known as Château des Vallons, is a château in Verteuil-d'Agenais, Lot-et-Garonne, France.

History

A castrum or small fort was documented there in 1259, around the motte. The village developed around the motte. From 1259 to 1390, the Château was in the possession of De Pis family. The Château was in a state of ruin at the end of the Hundred Years' War. It was given in 1460 to Aymeric of Caumont, Lord of Lauzun. He began the construction of the present château after 1460.
