Tours de Merle

Medieval castle in France · Saint-Geniez-ô-Merle, France

Photo: Stéfan Le Dû, CC BY-SA 2.5

About

The Tours de Merle are the ruins of a castle in the commune of Saint-Geniez-ô-Merle, in the Corrèze département of France. A feudal fortress from the twelfth and fifteenth centuries, which was subject of a classification as a historic monument since July 30, 1927.
