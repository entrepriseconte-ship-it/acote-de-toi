Château de Maintenon

Historical monument in France in Maintenon, in Eure-et-Loir, in France · Maintenon, France

Photo: Eric Pouhier, CC BY-SA 4.0

About

The Château de Maintenon is a château, developed from the original castle, situated in the commune of Maintenon in the Eure-et-Loir département of France. It is best known as being the private residence of Madame de Maintenon, the second spouse of Louis XIV.
