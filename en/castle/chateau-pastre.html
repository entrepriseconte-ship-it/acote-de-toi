Château Pastré

Museum in France · Marseille, France

Photo: Robert Valette, CC BY-SA 3.0

About

The Château Pastré, formerly known as the Château de Montredon, is a 19th-century building in the Montredon suburb in the south of Marseille, France. Originally the property of the wealthy merchant Pastré family, as of 2012, it housed a local faience pottery museum, the Musée de la Faïence de Marseille. The grounds of the château are a public park, known as the Campagne Pastré.
