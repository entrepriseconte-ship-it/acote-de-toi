About

Château de Laurens Castelet is a château in Benquet, Landes, Nouvelle-Aquitaine, France. It dates to 1872.

History

The estate was owned by Count Papin during the Revolution. He became a deputy in 1799 and died in 1809. His family lived there until 1855, when the property was in ruins. The Marquis de Cornulier bought the land, which included over thirty farms, and moved to the area in 1836. In 1855, the marquis built a simple house. After his death in 1862, his descendants built a castle in 1872, incorporating the old house as the left wing. The Cornulier family, who donated land for a new church in the 1880s, sold the estate in 1996.
