Palace of Poitiers

Merogvingian palace and seat of the Duchy of Aquitaine · Poitiers, France

Photo: Remi Jouan, CC BY-SA 3.0

About

The Palace of Poitiers was the palace of the Counts of Poitiers and subsequent Dukes of Aquitaine in Poitiers, in Poitou, western France. Its gothic architecture is notable as an example of the Plantagenet style.
