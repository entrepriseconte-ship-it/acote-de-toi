Château Rocher

French castle · Saint-Rémy-de-Blot, France

Photo: Matthieu Perona, CC BY 3.0

About

The Château Rocher is a French castle overlooking the Sioule river valley. It is located in the commune of Saint-Rémy-de-Blot in the Puy-de-Dôme département of the Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes région.
