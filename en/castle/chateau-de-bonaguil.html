Château de Bonaguil

Castle in Nouvelle-Aquitaine, France · Saint-Front-sur-Lémance, France

Photo: MOSSOT, CC BY-SA 3.0

About

The Château de Bonaguil is a castle in the French commune of Saint-Front-sur-Lémance, Lot-et-Garonne, owned by the neighbouring commune of Fumel. It has been classified a monument historique since 1862.
