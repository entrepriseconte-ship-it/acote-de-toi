Château Nairac

French wine · Barsac, France

Photo: Didier-CTP, CC BY-SA 4.0

About

Château Nairac is a sweet white wine ranked as Second Cru Classé in the original Bordeaux Wine Official Classification of 1855. Belonging to the Sauternes appellation in Gironde, in the region of Graves, the winery is located in the commune of Barsac.
