Musée du Petit Palais

Museum and art gallery in Avignon, southern France · Avignon, France

Photo: Herbert Frank from Wien (Vienna), AT, CC BY 2.0

About

The Musée du Petit Palais is a museum and art gallery in Avignon, southern France. It opened in 1976 and has an exceptional collection of "primitives" and early Renaissance paintings from Italy, which reunites those of the collection of Giampietro Campana deposed by the Musée du Louvre as well as paintings of the Avignon school deposed by the Musée Calvet. It is housed in a 14th-century building at the north side of the square overlooked by the Palais des Papes.
