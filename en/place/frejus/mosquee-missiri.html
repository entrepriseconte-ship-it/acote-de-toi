Missiri mosque

Mosque in France · Fréjus, France

Photo: Patricia.fidi, Public domain

About

The Missiri Mosque is a former French military community center inspired by sub-Saharan Islamic architecture. It was constructed in 1928–1930 for the Senegalese Tirailleurs based in military camps in Fréjus, in the southern France. Although its appearance evokes that of a mosque, its purpose and uses remained secular.
