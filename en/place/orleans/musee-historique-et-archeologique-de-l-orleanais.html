Musée historique et archéologique de l'Orléanais

French historical museum · Orléans, France

Photo: Carl Ha, CC BY-SA 4.0

About

The Orléanais Historical and Archaeological Museum is a local history museum at 22 rue de Charles Sanglier in Orléans. It was founded in 1823 by André Gaspard Parfait, count of Bizemont-Prunelé and assistant mayor of Orléans and includes the Neuvy-en-Sullias Hoard, a collection of medieval objects and other objects on the history of the city and area. It is a Museum of France.
