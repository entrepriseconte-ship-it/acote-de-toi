Mission of Full Gospel – Christian Open Door

Church located in Haut-Rhin, in France · Mulhouse, France

Photo: Strikehard, CC BY-SA 3.0

About

The Christian Open Door Church is a charismatic evangelical multi-site megachurch whose main place of worship is located in Mulhouse. It is the second largest Evangelical church in France, with over 2,200 people. The church is a member of the Federation of Full Gospel Churches in Francophonie and the National Council of Evangelicals of France. The senior pastor is Samuel Peterschmitt.
