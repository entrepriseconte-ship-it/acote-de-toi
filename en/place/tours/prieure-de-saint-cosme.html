Priory of St. Cosmas

Priory located in Indre-et-Loire, in France · Tours, France

Photo: Duch.seb, CC BY-SA 3.0

About

The Priory of St. Cosmas or the Priory of Ronsard is a former priory built upon an island in the river Loire at La Riche near Tours in Touraine, founded in the 11th century and dedicated to St. Cosmas. The site features a museum dedicated to the French poet Ronsard, who spent the last twenty years of his life among the Canons Regular there.
