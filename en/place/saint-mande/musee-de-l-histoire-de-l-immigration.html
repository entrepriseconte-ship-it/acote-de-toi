Cité nationale de l'histoire de l'immigration

Museum in Paris, France · Saint-Mandé, France

Photo: Jean-Pierre Dalbéra, CC BY 2.0

About

The Musée nationale de l'histoire de l'immigration is a museum of immigration history located in the 12th arrondissement of Paris at 293, avenue Daumesnil. The nearest métro station is Porte Dorée. It is open Tuesday through Friday from 10 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. and weekends from 10 a.m. to 7 p.m.; there is an admission fee.
