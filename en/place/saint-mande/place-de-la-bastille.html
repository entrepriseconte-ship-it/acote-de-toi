Place de la Bastille

Square in Paris, France · Saint-Mandé, France

Photo: Kaihsu, CC BY-SA 3.0

About

The Place de la Bastille is a square in Paris where the Bastille Prison once stood, until the storming of the Bastille and its subsequent physical destruction between 14 July 1789 and 14 July 1790 during the French Revolution. No vestige of the prison remains, although its footprint is marked by golden discs.
