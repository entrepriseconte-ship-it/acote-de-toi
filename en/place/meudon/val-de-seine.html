Val de Seine

Business district in Île-de-France · Meudon, France

Photo: User Metropolitan on en.wikipedia, Public domain

About

The Val de Seine is one of the most important business districts of the Paris agglomeration. Located southwest of the city, it spreads along a bend of the Seine, mainly in the municipalities of Boulogne-Billancourt and Issy-les-Moulineaux and in the 15th arrondissement of Paris.
