Metallic tower of Fourvière

Landmark in Lyon, France · Tassin-la-Demi-Lune, France

Photo: Corentin Eustacchi, CC BY-SA 4.0

About

The Tour métallique de Fourvière, a landmark of Lyon, France, is a steel framework tower which bears a striking resemblance to the Eiffel Tower, which predates it by three years. With a height of 101 m (331 ft), previously 85.9 m (282 ft) before installation of the TV broadcasting antenna, and weighing 210 tons, the "metallic tower" was built between 1892 and 1894.
