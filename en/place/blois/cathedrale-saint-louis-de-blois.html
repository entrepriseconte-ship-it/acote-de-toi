Saint Louis Cathedral of Blois

Catholic cathedral located in Loir-et-Cher, Centre-Val de Loire, France · Blois, France

About

Blois Cathedral, or the Saint Louis Cathedral of Blois, is a Late Gothic Catholic cathedral in Blois, Centre-Val de Loire, France. It has been a monument historique since 1906.
