Basilica of Saint-Ferjeux

Basilica located in Doubs, in France · Besançon, France

Photo: User:Arnaud 25, Public domain

About

The Basilica of St. Ferjeux is an eclectic Romano-Byzantine-style basilica situated in Besançon, in the quartier of Saint-Ferjeux. It is dedicated to the patron saints of Besançon, Ferreolus and Ferrutio. It honors both their spiritual lineage and the great saints who lived in Franche-Comté.
