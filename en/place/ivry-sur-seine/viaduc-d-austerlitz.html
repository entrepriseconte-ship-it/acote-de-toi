Viaduc d'Austerlitz

Bridge in Paris, France · Ivry-sur-Seine, France

Photo: David Monniaux, CC BY-SA 3.0

About

Viaduc d'Austerlitz is a single-deck, steel arch, rail bridge that crosses the Seine in Paris. It is used solely by Line 5 of the Paris Métro. It links Gare d'Austerlitz on the Rive Gauche to Quai de la Rapée on the other side of the river.
