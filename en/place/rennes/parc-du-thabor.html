About

The Parc du Thabor, is a 10-hectare (25-acre) near the city centre of Rennes, in Brittany, France. It has a mix of the characteristics of a French garden, an English garden and a large botanical garden. Its name refers to a mountain overlooking Lake Tiberias in Israel, Mount Tabor.
