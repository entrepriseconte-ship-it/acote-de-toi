About

Notre-Dame-du-Rosaire is a Roman Catholic church located at 194 Rue Raimond-Losserand in the Plaisance Quarter of the 14th arrondissement of Paris, France. It was built between 1910 and 1912 by architect Pierre Sardou in a combination of the styles of neo-classical architecture and neo-Byzantine architecture, a blend very popular in that period. In 1919 the architect received the Bally Prize from the Academy of Fine Arts for his design of the church.
