About

The Church of Saint-Antoine-de-Padoue is a Roman Catholic church located on Boulevard Lefebvre in the 15th arrondissement of Paris. Built by architect Leon Azema between 1933 and 1935, it is one of the best examples of Art Deco architecture and interior design in Paris.
