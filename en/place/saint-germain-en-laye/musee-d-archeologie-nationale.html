National Archaeological Museum

French National Archaeological Museum · Saint-Germain-en-Laye, France

Photo: KoS, CC BY-SA 3.0

About

The National Archaeological Museum is a major French archaeology museum, covering prehistoric times to the Merovingian period (450–750). It is housed in the Château de Saint-Germain-en-Laye in the département of Yvelines, about 19 kilometres (12 mi) west of Paris.
