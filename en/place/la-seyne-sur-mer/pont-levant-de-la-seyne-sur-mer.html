Pont basculant de la Seyne-sur-Mer

Bridge in France · La Seyne-sur-Mer, France

Photo: The original uploader was Clifden at French Wikipedia., CC BY-SA 2.0 fr

About

Pont basculant de la Seyne-sur-Mer is a former bascule bridge in La Seyne-sur-Mer in France. It was completed in 1918 and served originally as railway bridge. Today, it is permanently opened and used as 43-metre-tall observation tower.
