About

The Versailles Synagogue is a Jewish congregation and synagogue, located at 10, rue Albert Joly in Versailles in the Yvelines department, in the Île-de-France region of France. Built between 1884 and 1886 by the architect Alfred-Philibert Aldrophe, it was inaugurated in 1886, and is one of the oldest purpose-built synagogues in the Île-de-France region. Mrs Furtado Heine provided significant financial support to the establishment of the synagogue.
