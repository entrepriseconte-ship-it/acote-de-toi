Saint-Étienne-du-Mont

Church located in Paris, in France · Gentilly, France

Photo: Diliff, CC BY-SA 3.0

About

Saint-Étienne-du-Mont is a Catholic church in Paris, France, on the Montagne Sainte-Geneviève in the 5th arrondissement, near the Panthéon. It contains the shrine of St. Geneviève, the patron saint of Paris. The church also contains the tombs of Blaise Pascal and Jean Racine. Jean-Paul Marat is buried in the church's cemetery. Maurice Duruflé was titular church organist from 1929 to 1975.
