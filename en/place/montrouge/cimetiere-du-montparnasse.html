Montparnasse Cemetery

Cemetery in the Montparnasse quarter of Paris, France · Montrouge, France

Photo: Celette, CC BY-SA 4.0

About

Montparnasse Cemetery is a cemetery in the Montparnasse quarter of Paris, in the city's 14th arrondissement. The cemetery is roughly 19 hectares and is the second largest cemetery in Paris. The cemetery has over 35,000 graves, and approximately 1,000 people are buried there each year.
