Fort Gambetta

Castles & palaces · Metz, France

About

The Feste Hindersin renamed Gambetta fort by the French in 1919, is a military installation near Metz. It is part of the first fortified belt of the forts of Metz and had its baptism of fire in late 1944, when the Battle of Metz occurred.
