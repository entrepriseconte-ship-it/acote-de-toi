About

Châlons Cathedral is a Roman Catholic church in Châlons-en-Champagne, France, formerly known as Châlons-sur-Marne. Constructed between 1120 and 1635, Châlons Cathedral is considered to be one of the earliest true Gothic cathedrals built in France and has been listed as a Monument historique since 1862.
