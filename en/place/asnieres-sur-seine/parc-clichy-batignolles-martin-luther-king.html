Parc Clichy-Batignolles

Urban park in Paris, France · Asnières-sur-Seine, France

Photo: Contrepoints.Org, CC BY-SA 4.0

About

The Parc Clichy-Batignolles – Martin Luther King is a green space in Paris' 17th arrondissement (district). Part of the Clichy Batignolles urban development project, the park opened in stages between 2007 and 2021, and covers 10 hectares.
