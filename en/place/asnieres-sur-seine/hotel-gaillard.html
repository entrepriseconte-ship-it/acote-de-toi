About

The Hôtel Gaillard is a Parisian hôtel particulier built between 1878 and 1882 by architect Jules Février at the request of banker Émile Gaillard. It is located at 1, Place du Général-Catroux, in the 17th arrondissement of Paris. It has hosted since 2019 the Cité de l'économie et de la monnaie (Citéco), sponsored by the Bank of France, current proprietor of the building.
