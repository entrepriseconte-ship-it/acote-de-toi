St. Sava Church, Paris

Church located in Paris, in France · Aubervilliers, France

Photo: Nikola Smolenski, CC BY-SA 4.0

About

Saint Sava Serbian Orthodox Cathedral is an Eastern Orthodox church located in Paris, France. It is under jurisdiction of the Serbian Orthodox Eparchy of Western Europe of the Serbian Orthodox Church and serves as its cathedral church. It is dedicated to Saint Sava, the first Archbishop of the Serbian Orthodox Church.
