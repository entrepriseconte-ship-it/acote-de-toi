Fort Saint-Jean

French fortification from 1660 · Marseille, France

Photo: Robert Valette, CC BY-SA 3.0

About

Fort Saint-Jean is a fortification in Marseille, built in 1660 by Louis XIV at the entrance to the Old Port. Since 2013, it has been linked by two foot-bridges to the historical district Le Panier and to the Museum of European and Mediterranean Civilisations.
