About

The Château Borély is a château in the southern part of Marseille, France. Associated with Parc Borély and Borély Racecourse, it has been listed as a historical monument since 1936, and has housed the Museum of the Decorative Arts, Fashion and Ceramics since Marseille-Provence 2013 with its rich original decor.
