About

Lagny Abbey was a monastery in the present-day commune of Lagny-sur-Marne in the department of Seine-et-Marne in France, in the eastern suburbs of Paris. It was founded in 644, refounded in about 990 and after well over a millennium of existence was seized by the state during the French Revolution.
