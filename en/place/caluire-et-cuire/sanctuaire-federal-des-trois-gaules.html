Sanctuary of the Three Gauls

Religious structure in Lyon, France, dedicated to the imperial cult and used by the peoples of three provinces of Roman Gaul; known from fragments · Caluire-et-Cuire, France

Photo: Siren-Com, CC BY-SA 3.0

About

The Sanctuary of the Three Gauls (Tres Galliae) (French: Sanctuaire fédéral des Trois Gaules) was the focal structure within an administrative and religious complex established by Rome in the very late 1st century BC at Lugdunum (the site of modern Lyon in France). Its institution served to federalise and develop Gallia Comata as an Imperial province under Augustus, following the Roman conquest of Gaul carried out by Augustus's predecessor Julius Caesar.
