Old Mill of Vernon

Watermill in Vernon, France · Vernon, France

Photo: Spedona (Spedona), CC BY-SA 3.0

About

The Old Mill of Vernon is a former watermill built upon the remains of a medieval bridge in the Eure department of the Normandy region, France. The iconic half-timbered house stands on two piers of the former 12th-century bridge spanning the Seine and is considered the primary landmark of the city. The structure is listed in the French General Inventory of Cultural Heritage and has been part of a protected natural site since 1943.
