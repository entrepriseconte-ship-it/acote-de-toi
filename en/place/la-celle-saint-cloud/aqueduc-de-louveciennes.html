Aqueduc de Louveciennes

17th century aqueduct near Paris · La Celle-Saint-Cloud, France

Photo: TCY, CC BY-SA 3.0

About

The Aqueduc de Louveciennes, sometimes called the Aqueduc de Marly, is an aqueduct built in the 17th century under the reign of Louis XIV, located in Louveciennes. Now out of service, the aqueduct has been listed a monument historique since 1953. It was a part of the hydraulic network intended to provide water for the Château de Marly and the Gardens of Versailles from the Seine river, using a huge pump called the Machine de Marly.
