About

The Jardin des plantes de Nantes (Breton: Liorzh louzawouriezh Naoned) is a municipal botanical garden located on Rue Stanislas Baudry, Nantes, Loire-Atlantique, Pays de la Loire, France. It is open daily without charge, but a fee is charged for the greenhouses.
