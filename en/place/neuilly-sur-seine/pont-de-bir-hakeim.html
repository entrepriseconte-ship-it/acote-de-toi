Pont de Bir-Hakeim

Bridge over the Seine River in Paris, France · Neuilly-sur-Seine, France

Photo: Photo personnelle (own work), CC BY-SA 3.0

About

The Pont de Bir-Hakeim, is a steel open spandrel deck arch bridge on stone masonry starlings, which crosses the Seine in Paris, France. The bridge is named after the 1942 battle in Libya; until 1948 it was known as the Pont de Passy. The Pont de Bir-Hakeim connects the 15th and 16th arrondissements, passing over the northeast end of the Île aux Cygnes.
