Jardin d'Acclimatation

Amusement park in Paris, France · Neuilly-sur-Seine, France

Photo: Moktarama, CC BY 3.0

About

The Jardin d'Acclimatation is a 19-hectare (47-acre) children's amusement park in the northern part of the Bois de Boulogne in western Paris. Opened in 1860, it features a number of attractions.
