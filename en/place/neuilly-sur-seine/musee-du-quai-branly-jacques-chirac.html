Musée du quai Branly

Museum for the traditional art of Africa, Asia, Oceania, and the Americas in Paris, France · Neuilly-sur-Seine, France

Photo: Another one of my pictures: This photograph was taken by Med, CC BY-SA 4.0

About

The Musée du Quai Branly – Jacques Chirac is an art museum located in Paris, France. The museum, designed by French architect Jean Nouvel, features the indigenous art and cultures of Africa, Asia, Oceania, and the Americas. The museum collection comprises more than a million objects, of which 3,500 are on display at any given time in both permanent and temporary thematic exhibits. A selection of objects from the museum is also displayed in the Gallery of the Five Continents of the Louvre.
